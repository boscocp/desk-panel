# T9.5 — A soft alert before a meeting

Size: M · Prereqs: T9.1 · Files: `web/js/format.js`, `web/js/host.js`, `web/js/app.js`,
`web/themes/*/theme.{js,css}`, `web/sounds/chime.wav`, `scripts/make_chime.py`,
`server/providers_calendar.py`, `server/server.py`, `server/config.example.*`,
`android/.../MainActivity.java`, `docs/THEMING.md`

Requires: the phone on adb and a reachable PC, for the manual check

## Goal

Asked for on 2026-09-30: something visual and gentle 5 minutes before a meeting, and a soft
sound 1 minute before. The sound should be open-licensed and easy for a theme to replace, and
both times configurable.

## Steps

1. `agenda_soon_min` (5), `agenda_chime_min` (1) and `agenda_chime_volume` (0.4) in
   `config.toml`, validated at startup. They ride `/quotes` inside `agenda.alerts`, so no
   rebuild is needed to change them.
2. `agendaAlert(agenda, now)` in `format.js` gives the level (`soon` or `chime`), a per-meeting
   key and the volume. `agendaRows` marks the row with the bell.
3. `app.js` checks every second: `host.alert(level)` sets `data-alert`, and `host.chime(volume)`
   plays once per meeting, only while the panel is lit.
4. The sound is synthesised by `scripts/make_chime.py` (CC0). A theme may name its own file in
   `defineTheme`.
5. The WebView allows media without a gesture (`setMediaPlaybackRequiresUserGesture(false)`).
