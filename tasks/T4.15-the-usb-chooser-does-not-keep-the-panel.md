# T4.15 — MIUI's USB chooser does not keep the panel

Size: S · Prereqs: T4.11 · Files:
`android/app/src/main/java/dev/bosco/deskpanel/UsbCover.java`,
`android/app/src/test/java/dev/bosco/deskpanel/UsbCoverTest.java`,
`android/app/src/main/java/dev/bosco/deskpanel/PanelService.java`

Requires: the phone on adb, the USB switch, and a person to move it

## Goal

Reported from the chair on 2026-11-26: every move of the USB switch between the PC and the Mac
makes the phone ask what the USB connection is for. MIUI's Settings
(`UsbModeChooserReceiver`) opens `Settings$UsbDetailsActivity` on every `USB_STATE` with
`configured=true`, but only while the keyguard is unlocked. All twelve choosers the log kept
(2026-11-25 and 26) resumed with `isKeyguardLocked=false`: an online panel holds the screen on,
so a phone unlocked once stays unlocked all day. The chooser covered the panel until the screen
timed out behind it and T4.11 raised the panel, about a minute later.

Nothing in the repository changed this. No commit touches USB, and both computers' adb keys
are trusted (`dumpsys adb`), so it is not the RSA prompt. The chooser is MIUI's own, and no
setting turns it off.

## Steps

1. `UsbCover.reclaim(online, coveredAt, usbAt)`: online, and the panel's stop while interactive
   within 5 s of USB becoming configured, either order. Plain Java, JVM-tested.
2. `PanelService` records both times. A dynamic receiver for
   `android.hardware.usb.action.USB_STATE` (protected, `@hide` in the SDK) skips the sticky copy
   and records `connected && configured`. `panelCovered(true)` records the cover. Either one
   asks `UsbCover`, and a yes logs `usb=reclaim` (`Markers`) and calls `bringPanelToFront`.
   Both times are then zeroed, so one USB connect raises the panel at most once.

```bash
python scripts/check_acceptance.py
```

On the phone, with this build installed, a PC online, the phone unlocked with the panel in
front, and the switch moved away and back by a person:

```bash
adb logcat -c
timeout 180 sh -c 'until adb logcat -d -s DeskPanel | grep -q "usb=reclaim"; do sleep 3; done'
sleep 3
adb shell dumpsys activity activities | grep -q 'ResumedActivity.*dev.bosco.deskpanel'
```

The `adb logcat -c` runs before the switch moves, and adb drops while the switch is on the
other computer; the wait starts again once it is back.
