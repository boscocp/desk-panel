# T4.4 — Real screen sleep instead of brightness zero

Size: M · Prereqs: T4.3 · Files: `MainActivity.java`

## Goal

Let the screen genuinely sleep when the PC is off, and wake it when the PC returns. Better than
a black render on a lit display: real power saving, no burn-in, and actually dark.

This task **decides** whether [ADR 0005](../docs/adr/0005-real-screen-sleep.md) keeps its
primary design or falls back. Record the outcome in that ADR either way.

## Steps

1. Going offline: `clearFlags(FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON)` and let Android sleep on its own timeout.
2. Coming online:

   ```java
   setShowWhenLocked(true);
   setTurnScreenOn(true);
   getWindow().addFlags(WindowManager.LayoutParams.FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON);
   ```

   Both methods are API 27+ and **not deprecated**; the device is API 31. Do not reach for
   `FLAG_TURN_SCREEN_ON` or `ACQUIRE_CAUSES_WAKEUP` — deprecated in API 27 and 17 respectively.
3. Keep `screenBrightness` at `-1f` in both states now. Brightness is no longer the mechanism.
4. Keep emitting `screen=sleep` / `screen=wake`.

## Acceptance

- Shut the PC down: the phone screen **actually turns off**, not merely goes black. Confirm in
  a dark room and by the absence of backlight at an angle.
- Log in on the PC: the screen **comes on by itself**, without touching the phone and without
  stopping at the lock screen.

```bash
adb logcat -s DeskPanel   # screen=sleep then screen=wake
```

## Notes

- **If MIUI will not wake reliably**, revert to the T4.3 behaviour, mark the fallback as active
  in ADR 0005, and say why. That is a legitimate outcome, not a failure — the ADR exists to
  record exactly this.
- A device PIN may block `setShowWhenLocked`. Test both with and without a lock screen and
  document which combination works.
- This matters more once ErP Ready is off and the phone runs on battery while the PC sleeps
  ([ADR 0008](../docs/adr/0008-device-longevity.md)).
