# T6.12 — The right column becomes two cards, and the device line moves under the date

Size: M · Prereqs: T6.8, T6.10, T6.11 · Files: `web/themes/neon/theme.js`,
`web/themes/neon/theme.css`, `web/js/format.js`, `e2e/layout/measure.js`, `docs/THEMING.md`,
`e2e/layout/README.md`

Requires: Firefox on `PATH` for the layout checks. Nothing here needs the phone.

**Asked for from the chair on 2026-09-21**, in Portuguese, with the panel running, and it is
three complaints about one card:

1. the condition line repeats what the glyph beside the temperature already says, and costs a
   line of the panel's scarcest column;
2. the battery and the phone's temperature sit in the foot of the weather card, so **two
   temperatures share one box** and a reader has to work out which is the room and which is the
   handset;
3. the right column is one tall card for one forecast, and the calendar T9.1 will bring has
   nowhere to go.

## Goal

The right-hand column holds **two cards of equal height**: WEATHER above, and a reserved
AGENDA below, waiting for T9.1. The device line — battery, handset temperature, `na bateria` —
is a line under the date in the sidebar, beside the clock it belongs with. The weather card
holds the city, the glyph, the temperature and the day's range, and nothing else.

## Steps

1. **The condition text goes, and the label does not.** `renderWeather` drops `.w-cond`; the
   glyph takes the label as its accessible name (`role="img"`, `aria-label`) instead of
   `aria-hidden`. The words stay in `format.js`, still one per WMO code, still tested — seven
   glyphs cannot tell showers from rain, and the panel must not lose the only place that
   distinction is written down. `plain` keeps its line: it is the undesigned theme, and this is
   a decision about the designed one.

2. **`#battery` moves to the sidebar**, under `#date`, as a line in the flow. It stops being
   positioned against `#panel`, so `max-width: 222px` and the wrapping-with-a-cap argument go
   with it — the sidebar is a 296px column and the line is left-aligned in it like the date
   above. `#weather` loses `padding-bottom`, the foot that existed only to keep the two apart.

3. **The right column splits in two.** A wrapper in the grid's second track holds `#weather`
   and `#agenda` at `1fr 1fr`, so the split is exactly the middle whatever the left column's
   rows do. `#agenda` is a `.card` with a title and no content, marked `data-reserved` — the
   same promise `#shortcuts` makes, made visible because the owner asked to see the space.

4. **The harness learns what reserved means.** `measure.js` measures `#agenda` like any other
   section — it draws a border and a title, so it can collide — but a section carrying
   `data-reserved` is allowed to hold no text. Every other empty section is still the failure
   T6.1 added that check for.

5. **`agenda` joins the title tables** in both languages, which is what `format.test.js`'s
   key-set comparison then requires of the third language nobody has written yet.

6. **Re-measure, do not estimate.** The weather card is 372px tall today and becomes ~182.
   The stress fixture's two-line city, 48px temperature and 46px glyph were sized against the
   old height; whatever has to give, `check_layout.py` is what says so, in both themes and both
   languages. Write the measured numbers into the comments that carry the old ones — a stale
   measurement in a comment is how T6.8's arithmetic outlived the card it described.

```bash
python e2e/layout/check_layout.py --theme plain --lang en
python e2e/layout/check_scroll.py
python e2e/layout/check_pulse.py
! grep -rn "'w-cond'" web/themes/neon/theme.js
! grep -rnE '^\.w-cond' web/themes/neon/theme.css
! grep -rn 'padding-bottom: 62px' web/themes/neon/theme.css
grep -q "'agenda'" e2e/layout/measure.js
```

The three negative `grep`s are what a refactor would otherwise undo quietly: the condition line
coming back, its rule coming back with it, and the reserved foot outliving the thing it was
holding room for. They are written against the *use* rather than the word, because both files
say `.w-cond` in a comment explaining where it went.

Run the browser checks **one at a time**. They share a fixed Marionette port and a second run
attaches to the first run's Firefox rather than failing (`e2e/layout/README.md`).

## Manual check

On the device, at arm's length: the two right-hand cards read as two cards rather than as one
card with a rule through it, and the device line under the date reads as a caption of the
clock rather than as a fifth value. The one thing the harness cannot answer is whether the
glyph alone is enough to tell `Pancadas` from `Chuva` from a chair — that is the trade this
task makes, and the label is one line away in `format.js` if it turns out to be wrong.

## Notes

- **An empty card is a promise, not a placeholder.** The reserved AGENDA box says the panel has
  somewhere to put a meeting; if T9.1 slips far enough that the box is still empty in a month,
  the honest fix is to take the card back out, not to fill it with something invented.
- The STALE badge still sits over the weather card's top-right corner, which is still where
  `#panel` begins. T6.11's constraint — the badge and the longest card title have to fit that
  card's width between them — is unchanged, and `check_layout.py --lang` is still the only
  thing that says so.
