# T5.4 — Battery telemetry

Size: S · Prereqs: T5.1, TT.1 · Files: `PanelService.java`, `BatteryReading.java`,
`DataPayload.java`, `web/js/app.js`, `web/js/format.js`, `web/css/style.css`

Requires: phone (on adb)

## Goal

Surface battery level and **temperature** on the panel. Heat is what ages a lithium cell; better
to watch the number than to discover the problem as a swollen back cover.

## Steps

1. Register a receiver for `Intent.ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED` and read `EXTRA_LEVEL`,
   `EXTRA_SCALE`, `EXTRA_TEMPERATURE` (tenths of a degree Celsius) and `EXTRA_STATUS`.
2. Fold it into the existing `onData` payload, as the `battery` key in the T1.2 contract.
   The `window.onBattery(...)` alternative this task used to offer is **not** the design: a
   second bridge means a second thing to mock, a second thing to keep in step, and a render
   path the browser never exercises.
3. Render discreetly — this is diagnostic, not a headline. Small, in a corner.
4. Above **40 degrees**, show a visible but non-alarming warning.
5. Do not poll for this. The broadcast is push-based; polling it wastes exactly the power this
   task is about.

## Acceptance

```bash
node --test "web/test/**/*.test.js"
adb logcat -c && sleep 30 && adb logcat -d -s DeskPanel | grep -qE 'battery=[0-9]+'
```

The unit tests cover the formatting of level and temperature; the marker proves the broadcast
receiver is actually firing, which is the part that silently does not.

## Manual check

Level and temperature track reality — compare against Settings → Battery. Warm the phone
briefly and confirm the warning appears above 40 degrees.

## The DEVICE card is currently too loud, and the room is measured

Step 3 above says render discreetly, small, in a corner. T6.1 did not do that: `#battery` is a
full card with a `DEVICE` title, equal in weight to B3, FX, CRYPTO and WEATHER, which are the
content the panel exists for. Diagnostics sitting peer to content contradicts this task's own
step 3, so fixing it belongs here rather than in a layout task.

Measured with `e2e/layout/check_layout.py --extra-css`, at 872x392 under the stress payload.
Demoting DEVICE to a corner line and letting WEATHER span all three rows instead of two:

| | WEATHER box | free space in it | DEVICE |
|---|---|---|---|
| today | 224x256 | 29.4px | 224x108 card |
| demoted | 224x372 | 87.3px | 224x52 corner line |

Nothing else on the panel moves: B3, FX and CRYPTO keep h=140/108/108 with 6.9/6.3/6.3px free,
because `#panel`'s rows are fractional and the two columns are vertically independent.

**The point is WEATHER, not DEVICE.** WEATHER is the card about to receive real content from
T3.4 — a city name, a temperature, a min/max and labels as long as `Thunderstorm, heavy hail` —
and it is already the tightest thing on the panel in the worst case. Demoting DEVICE nearly
triples its headroom, at no cost to anything else. The fact that it also satisfies step 3 is the
smaller half of the reason.

Whoever does this: keep a size of at least 20px (T6.3's floor applies to the corner line too),
keep the >40C warning visible, and re-run the harness on both payloads. In the probe the corner
line came out with 0.0px to spare, which fits but leaves nothing.

## Notes

- **The receiver belongs to `PanelService`, not to `MainActivity`.** This task's header used to
  name the Activity, and the Activity is stopped for the whole offline stretch — so an
  Activity-scoped receiver would be unregistered precisely while the phone is running warm on a
  wake lock in a dark room, which is the case a thermometer is for. The service is alive
  whenever there is a payload to fold a reading into, which is the same condition.
- **Do not re-render the page on every broadcast.** `ACTION_BATTERY_CHANGED` fires on voltage
  and temperature movements that change nothing visible at one decimal place — measured on this
  device, roughly every eight seconds while charging — and `window.onData` is a full re-render
  of every card. `PanelService.publish()` compares the folded payload against what the page
  already has and stays quiet when they match.
- **"Charging" means the cable is in, and that is `EXTRA_PLUGGED`, not `EXTRA_STATUS`.** A phone
  reports `BATTERY_STATUS_NOT_CHARGING` whenever a charge is paused with power still connected —
  MIUI's charge optimisation, a thermal limit, or simply sitting at 100% on a charger — so
  `EXTRA_STATUS` puts "unplugged" on the panel with the cable plainly in. Step 1 above still says
  to read `EXTRA_STATUS`, and reading it is fine; deciding *this* bit from it is not.
- **A corner line that is positioned absolutely is not constrained by the column it looks like it
  is in.** `#battery` is positioned against `#panel`, which is the whole right side of the panel,
  so it needs an explicit `box-sizing: border-box` cap *and* wrapping; with a cap and no wrap the
  overflow just moves. `#weather` reserves the foot with `padding-bottom` rather than sharing it.
  Both were got wrong first time, and `check_layout.py` did not notice until it was taught to
  compare ink against ink.
- **The app cannot limit charging.** Android exposes no public API; `BatteryManager` is
  read-only. This is settled in [ADR 0008](../docs/adr/0008-device-longevity.md) — read it
  before being tempted to add a charge-limit feature.
- `EXTRA_TEMPERATURE` is in tenths: 315 means 31.5 degrees.
