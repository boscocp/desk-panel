# Wave 46 — The log names the PC it is on

A bug report from the chair, not a planned wave: "liguei o PC no CachyOS, depois o Mac,
desliguei o CachyOS, e trabalhei no Mac. Lá o app não mudou pro servidor do Mac, tive que fechar
o app e reabrir pra funcionar. Agora desliguei o Mac e vim pro CachyOS e o app não ligou."

Two complaints. The second one the evidence answered outright. The first one it could not — and
**that is what this wave is about**, because the reason it could not is a defect of its own.

## The second complaint was not a bug, and the timestamps say so

| Source | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| `uptime -s` | 10:19:59 | CachyOS booted |
| `systemctl --user show desk-panel ActiveEnterTimestamp` | 10:20:23 | the server came up with the session |
| server journal | 10:20:31 | first `GET /ping` 200 from the phone |
| `adb logcat -s DeskPanel` | 10:20:32 | `pc answered at <ip>`, `state=online`, `screen=wake` |

Eight seconds from the login signal to the lit panel, and the phone had been polling the ladder
at the 15 s cap throughout — `ping=err` at 10:19:23, :41, :59, 10:20:16. The panel was waiting
for a PC that had not finished booting. Invariant 2 working exactly as written.

## The first complaint is unanswerable, and that is the finding

The app's own process history says it was alive the whole time:
`dumpsys activity exit-info` has the previous process dying on **2026-10-09 14:30:47**, reason
2 (SIGNALED), with its WebView child killed as `SwipeUpClean` — the owner's own swipe, not a
MIUI kill. So from the CachyOS shutdown at 09:50 (`last`) to 14:30 the panel was running and
not following the Mac, and the restart appeared to fix it.

Nothing in the log can say why, because of how the two facts are written:

- `pc answered at <ip>` fires **on a move only**. With two PCs it is the only line that ever
  names a host.
- `probe failed: <host>: <reason>; …` fires **on the edge into OFFLINE only**.

Both are transition lines, and the question is asked hours later. The Redmi's `main` ring buffer
is 256 KiB, shared with every app, and this panel writes a `ping=` line every two seconds:
**about six minutes of history**, measured here. By the time anybody looks, the only thing left
is a wall of `ping=err` that cannot distinguish a PC that is off, a server that is not running,
a firewall dropping instead of refusing, or a cleartext address the pin does not cover.

ADR 0016 created the question "which PC is the panel on" and shipped no way to answer it. T4.5
has been `blocked` on this exact scenario — the move between two live PCs — since wave 35, so
the next time it happens the log has to be the record.

## What ran

**T4.8** on `wave/46-the-log-names-the-pc`:
- `PcHosts.Cycle` carries a `detail` token: `host=<ip>` when one answered (online or idle),
  `hosts=<ip>:<reason>,<ip>:<reason>` when none did, `hosts=not-attempted` for a cycle a stop
  ended before it dialled.
- `PcHosts.shortReason` is pure and bounded at 32 characters: the exception class without its
  package or message, anything undotted kept whole with its spaces closed up (`HTTP-401`). The
  two it exists to tell apart are `ConnectException` — nothing listening — and
  `SocketTimeoutException` — dropped rather than refused, which is the Linux firewall trap
  `SERVER-SETUP.md` ships no step for.
- `PcPoller` appends it to the `ping=` line. **Appended, never folded into the marker**:
  `MarkersTest.everyMarkerIsOneToken` asserts markers hold no spaces, and T5.2, T5.3 and T5.6
  all match `ping=` or `ping=ok` as a prefix, so every existing count is untouched.
- `docs/TESTING.md` gains the buffer step. `adb logcat -b main -G 4M` was applied to this
  device; it is lost on reboot and `persist.logd.size` is refused to `shell`, so it is a
  per-session step and not a fix.

8 new JVM tests, 23 in `PcHostsTest`, 143 on the JVM, all green. One was sabotaged —
`brief.append(failure)` instead of `shortReason(failure)` — and `noAnswerAnywhereNamesEveryHost
AndWhyItFailed` went red alone, so the assertion can fail.

## What this wave deliberately did not do

**It changed no behaviour.** No defect was found in the following itself, and the review of
`PcHosts.cycle`, `PcPoller`, `DataPoller` and `SpectrumStream` found all four reading
`hosts.active()` per cycle as ADR 0016 says they should. Today's log proves the two-host cycle
runs in the installed build: 18 s between `ping=err` lines is the 15 s cap plus two probes.

Adding a guess — a periodic forced re-probe of every host, say — would have been a behaviour
change aimed at a cause nobody has seen. The honest move is to make the next occurrence legible
and leave T4.5 blocked until it is.

## What is still only true on this desk

- **The Mac's side is unexamined.** It was off all session. Wave 45's note records a declined
  permission prompt at login on the Mac the same morning, and that session's own check found
  `/ping` answering 200 by the time it looked. Whether the Mac's agent was reachable from the
  phone during those 4h40m is the one fact that would settle the first complaint, and it is on
  the Mac, not here.
- **The APK on the Redmi is current with `main` for `android/` and `web/`** — `git diff
  a864fd2..HEAD -- android/ web/` is empty, so wave 45 was Mac-side only, as its note says.
- The phone reports `USB powered: true` with the PC off, so this desk's panel is never dormant
  (T5.6) — the ladder, not the alarm, is what recovers it. Worth knowing before reading any
  assertion about `dormant=`.
- `make check` is still red on `lint-commits` for main's `d5c4e95`, as wave 45 recorded. It
  ages out after 30 commits.
