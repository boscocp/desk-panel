# Wave 49 — A covered panel comes back

Reported from the chair: after moving the peripherals from CachyOS to the Mac with the USB
switch, the panel's screen went out and never came back. The owner asked for an investigation
and a fix, and offered a Claude session on the CachyOS box. It was not needed: the phone was on
adb at the Mac, and the cause was entirely on the phone.

## The diagnosis

The log had no `state=` or `screen=` line after `pc answered at 192.168.15.3` at 11:28, and a
`data=ok` every minute. So the app was online and the phone was `Dozing`. Both servers answered
`{"ok": true, "display": "on"}`. The fault was between the app and the screen.

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 13:26:52 | Play Services starts `octarine.ui.OctarineWebviewActivity`, then `OctarineActivity`, over the panel (a Google account screen) |
| afterwards | the screen times out behind it: `FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON` holds only while the panel's window is showing |
| 14:44–14:51 | four `Waking up … WAKE_REASON_PLUGGED_IN`, each followed 20 s later by `Going to sleep due to application`: the USB switch re-plugging the phone's cable, waking it onto the lock screen over Play Services |
| 14:52 | `dumpsys activity`: the gms task `visible=true` on top, the panel's `visible=false` under it |

The switch was what the owner saw, not the cause. Nothing in the app raised the panel again,
because a PC that stays online makes no transition, and `bringPanelToFront` hangs off the
transition.

## What ran

**T4.11** on `wave/49-panel-reclaims-front`: `MainActivity.onStop` while the device is still
interactive marks the panel covered, and `onStart` clears it. `PanelService` listens for
`ACTION_SCREEN_OFF`; online and covered, it takes the online wake's lock and raises the panel,
and `setTurnScreenOn` relights the screen.

**Measured on the Redmi**, with Settings opened over the panel and the sleep key:

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| covered, `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` ignore | refused (`allowBackgroundActivityStart: false`), stays `Dozing`, logs `cannot raise it without Display over other apps` |
| covered, allowed | `panel was covered; brought back`, `WAKE_REASON_APPLICATION … handleTurnScreenOn`, `Awake`, panel focused |
| panel in front | the receiver does nothing. The screen relit within 0.5 s anyway, through the window's own `setTurnScreenOn`. That is older behaviour, not this wave's |

## The permission had been off for six days

`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` read `ignore` with its last change six days old: wave 48's install. The
first run of the fix was refused for that reason, and this wave's own `adb install -r` reset it
to `ignore` again. INSTALL-PHONE claimed only the two MIUI ops reset on install. It now names
all three, and the receiver checks `canDrawOverlays` rather than logging success for a start
Android dropped silently.

## What is not proven

- A real screen timeout over Play Services was not observed with the fix; the sleep key sends
  the same `ACTION_SCREEN_OFF`.
- With the permission off the receiver returns before taking the wake lock. If Android refuses
  the start for another reason, the lock stays held until a window gains focus, exactly as it
  does after a refused online wake. That path was not provoked.
