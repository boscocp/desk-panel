# Wave 47 — The first PC listed comes first

A bug report from the chair: "liguei antes desse update aqui e o celular não ligou", and then,
about the day before, "quando liguei o Mac, tava no CachyOS o servidor e não trocou pra cá".
These were two separate causes, and neither was the one wave 46 was built to catch.

## This morning: the app was not running

`dumpsys activity exit-info` and the `events` buffer show the same kill:

| Time | What |
|---|---|
| 00:57 → 03:18:57 | `ping=idle host=192.168.3.97` every ~3.5 s: on the Mac, Mac display off — correct |
| 03:18:58.443 | `am_kill … dev.bosco.deskpanel … stop com.google.android.webview due to installPackageLI` |
| 03:18:58 | `com.google.android.webview` `lastUpdateTime`, version 154.0.8037.107 |
| 03:18:58 → 09:20 | no `DeskPanel` line at all |
| 09:20:50 | launched by hand with `am start`: `state=online`, `screen=wake` on the Mac |

A Play Store WebView update kills every app that uses a WebView. `START_STICKY` did not bring the
service back, and nothing else in the app could. **That is T4.10, filed and not done.** The device-side
mitigation is to turn off auto-update for Android System WebView.

## Yesterday: the panel was doing what ADR 0016 says

With the panel on CachyOS and the Mac switched on, staying on CachyOS was the documented "no
flapping" rule: move only when the active host stops answering. The log for it was gone (the
process had restarted since), but no log was needed: the rule produces exactly that report.
The owner decided against the rule: "priority always PC, but if PC server went down, change to
MAC if MAC is on."

## What ran

**T4.9** on `wave/47-the-pc-comes-first`, [ADR 0022](../adr/0022-the-first-pc-comes-first.md):

- `PC_IP` order is priority. While on a lower host, one cycle in `PcHosts.PRIORITY_EVERY` (5)
  asks in listed order, and the rest stay active-first.
- **Why not every cycle:** measured from the phone, a probe to the PC while it is off is a
  timeout, not a refusal (`nc -w 3` → 3.05 s; `TIMEOUT_MS` makes it 1.5 s). Every cycle would
  have taken the 2 s online cadence on the Mac to about 3.5 s.
- A dark PC still falls through (ADR 0020), so a PC whose monitor slept leaves the panel on the Mac.

7 new JVM tests, 150 on the JVM, all green. Sabotage: making the priority order never apply
turned two of them red (`theFirstListedPcTakesThePanelBackOnAPriorityCycle`,
`onlyOneCycleInPriorityEveryPaysForAPcThatIsOff`).

Release APK built and installed on the Redmi, and the app-ops and `deviceidle` entry were
set again. On the Mac with the PC off: `pc answered at 192.168.3.97`, `state=online`, `data=ok`,
and 31 `ping=ok` in 75 s, about 2.4 s a cycle, which is the one-in-five cost.

## What the review found

One fresh-context review pass. It found no blocking defect, and every finding was applied:

| Severity | Finding | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Both PCs on, the PC misses a probe (the T4.7 game case): the panel falls over to the Mac and comes back within five cycles. Presses and data in that window go to the Mac. ADR 0016 moved once and stayed. | This is what a priority order costs. It is written into ADR 0022's consequences, not coded around. The state stays online, so the screen is untouched. |
| Medium | `display.py` answers `"unknown"` on KWin and sway, which counts as on. On a KDE session the PC would keep the panel even with its monitor asleep. | Written into ADR 0022. Which desktop this CachyOS runs is not verified. |
| Low | Nothing pinned the counter reset: a PC reached mid-count and then lost would be asked first again within two cycles. | `afterFallingOverTheFirstPcWaitsAFullRoundBeforeItIsAskedFirst`. Removing the reset turned it red, alone. |
| Low | Nothing pinned "two dark PCs settle on the first listed, once". | `twoDarkPcsSettleOnTheFirstListedAndStay`. |
| Low | Stale text: ADR 0020's "stays on the active PC", ADR 0016's "each cycle asks that host first", BUILD.md's "whichever answered last", and a `PcHosts` comment that contradicted its own code. A straggler thread could touch the counter, too. | All four texts corrected. The counter is now `volatile`. |

## What is not proven

- **The take-back itself has not been seen on the desk.** The PC was off all session; the JVM
  tests are the only evidence that the PC wins within five cycles.
- The APK on the Redmi was built before the review edits. Those edits changed comments, tests
  and a `volatile`, with no behaviour change, so it was not reinstalled.
- `adb logcat -b main -G 4M` was applied and is lost on reboot.
