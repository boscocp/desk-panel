# Wave 52 — The panel comes back after a reboot

The owner logged into the PC on 2026-11-04 and the panel did not come on. They asked what
happened, then for the permanent fix.

## The investigation

The PC side was healthy. `desk-panel.service` had been active since 12:25:02. The phone had
`sys.boot.reason` `shutdown,userrequested`, an uptime of 3 minutes at 12:27, no app process,
the MIUI launcher resumed, and no log line from the app since the boot. Autostart was granted
(`MIUIOP(10008): allow`). The manifest had no boot receiver. `PHONE-SETUP.md` said that was
deliberate and that the vendor's Autostart list does the job, and `INSTALL-PHONE.md` said the
panel returns within two minutes of a reboot. T2.4, the task that measures that, had been
`blocked` since 2026-09-16. Autostart is a permission to deliver the broadcast, and there was
nothing to deliver it to.

`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW` was also `ignore`, five days after the last install, the fourth time
for wave 50's open question.

## What ran

**T4.14** on `wave/52-boot-receiver`:

- `BootReceiver`, not exported, starts `PanelService` on `BOOT_COMPLETED` with `ACTION_BOOT`.
  The service logs `service started at boot` and raises the panel when the PC answers, as a
  watchdog revival does.
- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` in the manifest.
- ADR 0025. ADR 0023, `PHONE-SETUP.md`, `INSTALL-PHONE.md` and T2.4 no longer say that
  Autostart brings the app back.
- `scripts/check_acceptance.py` accepts `adb logcat -G`, which sets the buffer and exits like
  `-d` and `-c`. It had flagged the task's buffer line as a command that never returns.

**Measured on the Redmi.** The planned `adb reboot` showed the app silent for 90 s after
`sys.boot_completed`. The cause was not the code: the phone has a PIN, the user stayed
`RUNNING_LOCKED`, and only `LOCKED_BOOT_COMPLETED` had gone out. ADR 0025's first draft said the
development phone had no secure lock screen. That was assumed, not read, and it was wrong.
While the owner unlocked, the phone rebooted itself (`reboot,rollback_staged_install`,
09:24). That reboot became the test. Unlocked at about 09:29:40, `BOOT_COMPLETED` went out at
09:29:45, `Start proc ... for broadcast {dev.bosco.deskpanel/.BootReceiver}` at 09:30:27, then
`Background started FGS: Allowed`, `service started at boot`, `state=online`, and `screen=wake`
at 09:30:29, with `MainActivity` resumed. The device acceptance lines after the unlock exit 0.
`./gradlew test` and `make check` are green.

## What the review found

One fresh-context review pass, before the device run. No High findings.

| Severity | Finding | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | `adb wait-for-device` plus `sleep 120` races a slow MIUI boot | Fixed: the acceptance waits for `sys.boot_completed`, and later for the unlock and the log line. |
| Medium | The 256 KiB main buffer, reset at boot, can lose the line before the grep | Fixed: `adb logcat -b main -G 4M` after the boot. |
| Low | A force-stopped app (recents clear, MIUI cleaner) gets no `BOOT_COMPLETED`, which looks like the original bug | Documented in ADR 0025, ADR 0023 and INSTALL-PHONE step 1. The acceptance checks `stopped=false`. |
| Low | An update kills the process and nothing restarts it (no `MY_PACKAGE_REPLACED`) | Documented, declined: it predates this wave, and an install is done by hand. |
| Low | The manifest's service comment said only `MainActivity` starts it | Fixed. |
| Low | T2.4 still framed reboot survival as Autostart's job | Points at T4.14 now. |
| Low | `grep BOOT_COMPLETED` matched the permission line too | Now greps the action and `.BootReceiver`. |
| Low | `service started at boot` is not logged if the app was opened before the broadcast | Declined: behaviour is the same, and the acceptance says not to open the app. |
| Nit | ADR 0025 named only Android 12's exemption | Added Android 15's boot-restricted FGS types, `specialUse` not among them. |
| Nit | The log line is inline, not in `Markers` | Declined: same as `service revived by watchdog`, which is not a state transition. |

## Where the rework went

- **The ADR asserted a fact about the phone that nobody had checked.** "The development phone has
  no secure lock screen" was written from memory of nothing. `dumpsys user` would have said
  `RUNNING_LOCKED` in one command. The device run caught it, the reviewer did not, because the
  reviewer had no phone.
- **The first `adb install` was refused** (`INSTALL_FAILED_USER_RESTRICTED`): MIUI wants a tap on
  the phone. The owner had to come back for it. INSTALL-PHONE already says so, in its vendor
  detail.

## What is not proven

- **A reboot with no PIN** was not run. ADR 0025 expects the panel within a minute or two of the
  boot, untouched. It rests on the same broadcast, sent earlier.
- **A planned reboot through to the panel** was not run end to end: the planned one stopped at
  the PIN, and the unplanned one finished the test.
- `BATTERY_CHANGED` logged `Permission Denial ... DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` at
  the service's start. It was seen once, it predates this wave, and the battery line on the
  panel was not checked against it.
- What switches "Display over other apps" off is still unknown.

## Cost

Five prompts from the owner, two of them to unlock the phone and one to approve the install.
Tokens and wall clock were not measured.
