# Wave 53 — Stay awake is checked, and the USB chooser does not keep the panel

Two reports from the chair on 2026-11-26. The PC's monitor went to sleep, and the phone dimmed
on the lock screen instead of sleeping. And every move of the USB switch between the PC and
the Mac makes the phone ask what the USB connection is for.

## What ran

**T4.16** and **T4.15** on `wave/53-usb-switch-and-stay-awake`.

- **Stay awake.** The app had logged `state=idle` and `screen=sleep` at 17:00:56 and released
  its flags. The display stayed on for 85 minutes, until a hand pressed power at 18:25. Cause:
  `stay_on_while_plugged_in=7`, which ADR 0005 forbids and nothing checked. It was set back to
  0 over adb. `scripts/check_phone_settings.py` and `make phone-settings` now fail on it, and
  `install-phone` and `e2e` depend on that target. Proved by setting 7 (exit 1) and then 0 (exit
  0). On 2026-11-27, `screen=sleep` at 09:54:16 was followed by `Going to sleep due to timeout`
  10 s later.
- **The USB prompt.** It is not the RSA prompt: `dumpsys adb` lists both hosts' keys. It is not
  a repository change either: no commit touches USB. It is MIUI's `UsbModeChooserReceiver`,
  which opens `Settings$UsbDetailsActivity` on `USB_STATE configured=true` only while the
  keyguard is unlocked. All 12 choosers the log kept resumed with `isKeyguardLocked=false`. A
  panel online keeps the screen on, so a phone that was unlocked once stays unlocked. `UsbCover`
  (JVM-tested) treats a cover within 5 s of a USB connect as the chooser, and `PanelService`
  raises the panel and logs `usb=reclaim`.
- Found on the way: `make e2e` runs `e2e/run_e2e.py`, which does not exist. Left as found, and
  written down below.

## What the review found

One fresh-context pass over the uncommitted diff. Five findings, all applied:

| Severity | Finding | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | Zeroing only the cover time did not consume anything: `onStart` zeroes it anyway, and `usbConnectedAt` stayed live, so every new cover in the 5 s window was raised again. That could fight a person reopening their app, or a chooser shown again | Both times are zeroed on a reclaim, so there is one raise per connect. The comment's claim was false and is rewritten |
| Low | The acceptance grepped an inline log string | The marker is now `Markers.usbChooserReclaim()` = `usb=reclaim`, in MarkersTest's no-space list |
| Low | INSTALL-PHONE said the setting "came back on by itself" and that scrcpy restores it "only on a clean exit"; neither was checked | Reworded: found on as `7`, scrcpy's value, cause not confirmed |
| Low | `! check_phone_settings.py` also passes on exit 2 (no phone) | The command is now `...; test $? -eq 1` |
| Low | Every adb failure read as "no phone" and dropped adb's stderr | adb's own error is carried into the message |

## What is not proven

- **T4.15 has not run on the device.** The one switch move after the install, 2026-11-27
  09:57:51, came with the keyguard up. MIUI then started its service instead of the chooser, so
  there was nothing to reclaim, and the owner saw no prompt for that reason. One move with the
  phone unlocked and the panel in front is the check.
- The false positive (a person opening an app within 5 s of a replug) is reasoned, not
  measured.
- Why Stay awake was on is unknown.
