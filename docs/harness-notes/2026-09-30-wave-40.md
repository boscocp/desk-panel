# Wave 40 — meeting alerts, the volume bar, and humidity

Three requests from the chair, arriving one after another while the wave ran, in Portuguese:
- a soft visual and audible alert before a meeting, with a replaceable open sound and
  configurable times;
- "do lado do botão do mic quero uma barra pra controlar o som geral do computador";
- the relative humidity under the day's range.

After those came six refinements, each made on the device while the owner watched the panel:
the bar twice as tall with an icon, a stronger pulse, a "!" beside the title, the "!" 4 px
smaller, the bar taller again, and the bar centred.

## What ran

**T9.5, the meeting alerts.**
- `agenda_soon_min` / `agenda_chime_min` / `agenda_chime_volume` ride `agenda.alerts`, and
  `agendaAlert` in `format.js` decides.
- `host.js` sets `data-alert` and plays the chime once per meeting.
- The sound is synthesised by `scripts/make_chime.py`, so its licence is not a question, and a
  theme can name its own file.
- Measured on the Redmi against the real server with a fake meeting: the bell, the pulse and the
  "!", and the app's AudioTrack started about a minute before the start. The owner heard it.

**T8.4, the volume bar.**
- It needed ADR 0015 to allow a value, so the owner was asked, and chose the real slider over
  step buttons. That was the wave's one question.
- `POST /action/volume/<0-100>` is parsed to an int before anything touches the mixer.
- `wpctl`/`pactl`, AppleScript, and PowerShell against IAudioEndpointVolume slots 4 and 6.
- The phone relays it through a typed `setVolume(int)`.
- Measured: a tap on the Redmi moved the Mac from 100 to 40.

**T6.16, humidity.** Open-Meteo's `relative_humidity_2m`, checked live first (it answered 40),
drawn as a drop and a number. Verified on the Redmi.

Results: `make check` exit 0; server tests 570; web tests 164.

## What the review found

One fresh-context agent reviewed the diff read-only and found no security defect: no request
text reaches a command, and the JavaScript string rebuilds the level from an int. It made two
medium findings and five low ones, and all were applied:

- **A background volume read could overwrite a newer set.** A generation counter now drops the
  older answer, and a test holds the read open across a set to prove it.
- **Windows would compile PowerShell every 20 s** for as long as the session is logged in. The
  TTL is 120 s on Windows.
- The plain slider stopped following the PC once it held focus.
- `pactl` now reports the loudest channel.
- A Javadoc had been orphaned, and three comments and an ADR sentence claimed things the code
  does not do.

## What is not proven

- The volume bar on **Windows and CachyOS**. The Windows vtable was read by review, not run.
- A chime from a **real** calendar meeting, and one at night.
- The alert on the plain theme on the device. It was checked in headless Chrome only.
- Firefox's `check_layout.py` has not run for any of this. Headless Chrome and the device
  screenshots are the evidence.

## Cost

Ten owner messages; tokens were not measured. There was one subagent, the review. About a dozen
APK rebuilds and installs, one per adjustment from the chair.
