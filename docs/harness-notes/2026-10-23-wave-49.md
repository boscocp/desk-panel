# Wave 49 — A refused wake is asked for again

The owner reported from the chair that the monitor and the phone had slept for inactivity, and
the phone did not come back with the PC. They asked for an investigation, then for a fix,
review and merge.

## The investigation

The phone was on adb and its log still held the morning.

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 12:15:25 | `state=online`, `screen=wake`, `Waking up from Dozing ... handleTurnScreenOn`, `panel=rendered` |
| 12:25:15 | `state=idle`, `screen=sleep` |
| 13:35:23.025 | `state=online` |
| 13:35:23.093 | `screen=wake` |
| 13:35:23.153 | `W ActivityTaskManager: Background activity start [... allowBackgroundActivityStart: false; ... inVisibleTask: false]` |
| 13:37 | `mWakefulness=Dozing`, `ping=ok` every 2 s |

`appops` had `SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW: ignore`. On 10-20 the log read `Background activity start
... allowed because SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, and the package had not been updated since 10-19.
Something on the phone revoked it, and the log does not say what. The two earlier wakes that
day landed without it, because Android let them through as an activity in a visible task.
Re-granting it and sending `KEYCODE_WAKEUP` brought the panel back by hand.

## What ran

**T4.11**, [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-a-refused-wake-is-asked-again.md):

- `PanelService.bringPanelToFront` books a check 30 s out, unless the panel is already showing.
  `WakeRetry.next(online, interactive)` decides: `STOP` offline, `SETTLED` on a lit display,
  `RETRY` on a dark one. `RETRY` logs `wake=retry` and asks again.
- Every request logs `overlay=denied` when `Settings.canDrawOverlays` is false.
- `MainActivity` reports focus both ways. Focus settles a wake only on a lit display.

**Measured on the Redmi.** On demand, short dark stretches were let through by Android every
time, even with the launcher on top and MIUI's op 10021 denied. Wave 48's path refuses
deterministically, so the run used it. The grant was denied, then
`am force-stop com.google.android.webview` ran at 16:34:04, and the watchdog revived the service
at 16:58:08. The Mac's display was off until 17:42:

| Time | Line |
|---|---|
| 17:42:09 | `state=online`, `overlay=denied`, `Abort background activity starts` |
| 17:42:39 | `wake=retry`, `overlay=denied`, refused |
| 17:43:09 | `wake=retry`, `overlay=denied`, refused |
| 17:43:23 | grant restored with `appops` |
| 17:43:39 | `wake=retry`, `allowed because SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permission is granted` |
| 17:43:40 | `screen=wake`, `Waking up from Dozing`, `panel=rendered` |

That run used the pre-review build. The final build was then installed and woke normally with
the grant in place. It logged no `wake=retry` in the 50 s after the wake, and the wake lock was
released at focus (17:45:12).

## What the review found

One fresh-context review pass over the diff, before the phone run finished:

| Severity | Finding | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | `panelVisible` cancelled the check on focus alone, which the ADR itself says is no proof of a wake. Focus regained on a dozing display would drop the check and the lock, which is the 10-23 symptom with nothing logged. | Fixed: focus settles only with `isInteractive()`. |
| Low | A request made while the panel was already up booked a check; a power-key press inside those 30 s would be retried and relight the screen. | Fixed: such a request books no check and settles at once. |
| Low | Retries never ended over another app on a lit display, with the lock held, and would pull the panel over Settings the moment the grant came back. | Fixed: only a dark display is retried; a lit one settles. |
| Low | TESTING.md said a `screen=wake` without a retry is a wake that landed, which is false if the PC leaves within 30 s; and called `overlay=denied` per-cycle. | Reworded. |
| Low | README and INSTALL-PHONE claimed the next retry lights the panel, unmeasured at the time. | Measured since, at 17:43:39. |
| Nit | "Two a minute" counted retries, not requests. | Reworded. |

## What is not proven

- The original case, a wake refused after a long dark stretch with the grant missing, was not
  reproduced. The retry was proven on the watchdog path, where the start is refused for the
  same reason.
- What revoked the grant on the phone is unknown.
- The final build's retry was not re-run through a full refusal; the logic for a dark display
  is the same as in the build that was.
