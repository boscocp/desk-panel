# Wave 48 — A watchdog brings the panel back

The owner asked, after wave 47, whether T4.10 could be fixed in code, then said go ahead.

## The kill, reproduced

`adb shell am force-stop com.google.android.webview` kills the app the same way the 03:18 Play
Store update did:

| | 03:18:58 (Play) | 12:53:37 (repro) |
|---|---|---|
| `exit-info` reason | 10 (USER REQUESTED) | 10 (USER REQUESTED) |
| description | `stop com.google.android.webview due to installPackageLI` | `stop com.google.android.webview due to from pid 21589` |
| back on its own | no, for six hours | no, after 90 s |

`dumpsys package` still said `stopped=false` afterwards. That was the deciding fact: the kill is
aimed at WebView, so this app's alarms survive it.

## What ran

**T4.10**, [ADR 0023](../adr/0023-a-watchdog-brings-the-service-back.md):

- `PanelService` re-arms a 15 min `setAndAllowWhileIdle` alarm on every `onStartCommand`. It
  targets itself through `PendingIntent.getForegroundService(ACTION_WATCHDOG)` and is cancelled
  in `onDestroy`, which a deliberate stop reaches and a kill does not.
- The background start relies on the `deviceidle` allowlist, which INSTALL-PHONE step 6 already
  sets after every install.
- A revival logs `service revived by watchdog`.

**Measured on the Redmi:**
- The alarm was listed as `*walarm*:dev.bosco.deskpanel.WATCHDOG` with a +10 min inexact
  window, and it was still listed after the kill.
- The app was killed at 12:59:49. `service revived by watchdog` came at 13:27:05 with nobody
  touching the phone. That is 27 minutes from the kill. The alarm was armed at launch, about 20 s
  earlier, so it fired at the end of its window: 15 min plus the 0.75 × 15 min inexact slack.

## What the review found

One fresh-context review pass. It found no code defect and confirmed there are no PendingIntent
collisions. A refused background start is dropped by the system, not thrown into the app. Its
findings were all about the ADR claiming more than was measured:

| Severity | Finding | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | "A swipe now brings the panel back" was false: a swipe that finishes the Activity reaches `stopIfUnclaimed` → `onDestroy`, which cancels the watchdog. "A MIUI memory kill" is covered only if it leaves `stopped=false`, which nobody has measured. | Both reworded to say what is known. |
| Low | "MIUI autostart covers a reboot" has nothing in the code behind it: there is no boot receiver. | Reworded. Whatever brings the panel back after a reboot also re-arms the watchdog, and that was not re-measured. |
| Low | The inexact window bound is 0.75 × 15 min, not the 10 min observed once. | Manual check says about 26 min. The measured revival at 27 min is the alarm being armed ~20 s before the kill, plus that bound. |
| Low | "Not deferred in Doze" holds only with the allowlist. | Said so in the ADR, with INSTALL-PHONE's three-day measurement. |

## The revival found a second fault, on the Mac

The revived panel said `state=offline` with the Mac in use: `192.168.3.97:IOException`,
`unexpected end of stream`. The Mac's server answered `127.0.0.1` and dropped every LAN
connection with `OSError: [Errno 57] Socket is not connected`. The last LAN `/ping` 200 was at
12:53:35. Homebrew had upgraded `python@3.14` at 09:23, and its cleanup deleted the old keg at
about 12:53. The firewall's allow rule named that keg's `Python.app`, ad-hoc signed, and the
server process was still running the deleted binary. Restarting the agent picked up `python3`
→ 3.15, which has no rule, so the LAN stays blocked until the owner adds one with `sudo`.

That is **T3.14**, filed. It is also the first concrete candidate for wave 46's unexplained "the
panel did not move to the Mac".

## What is not proven

- A MIUI memory kill and a swipe that kills before `onDestroy` were not reproduced.
- After the revival, the panel was not seen bringing itself to the front: the Mac was blocked,
  so it never went online. `bringPanelToFront` is the same path a wake uses, but a cold start
  from an alarm was not observed reaching it.
- A reboot was not tested.
